## 0.4.0

A new GoMessages. Instead of showing the Google Messages web page in a
window, it's now a full desktop app with its own interface, connected to
your phone directly.

**Pair once more after updating**: choose **Sign in with Google** and tap
the matching emoji on your phone.

New:

- A desktop interface: archive, search, photos and files, reactions,
  replies, deleting messages, typing indicators, dual SIM, and new chats
  from your contacts.
- Pairing without Bluetooth or QR codes, on Mac, Windows, and Linux.
- `Cmd+K` to jump to any conversation, and a shortcut for every action.
- Eight [themes](/GoMessages/themes/), light and dark, with optional
  transparency.
- Notifications with an optional preview, and mute per conversation.

Changed:

- The unread count shows in the window title.
- Closing the window quits the app.
- Linux needs GTK 4 and WebKitGTK 6.0.
- GoMessages is now licensed under the AGPL-3.0.

Not in this version yet: the menu bar and tray icon, keeping the app running
with the window closed, and the Dock badge.
