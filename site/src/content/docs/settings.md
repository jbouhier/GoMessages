Open with `Cmd+,` (Mac) or `Ctrl+,` (Windows, Linux). On a Mac it's also
**GoMessages → Settings…** in the menu bar.

<img src="/GoMessages/product/settings.jpg" alt="GoMessages settings" width="2400" height="1500" />

## Appearance

- **Mode**: System follows your computer's light or dark setting. Light and
  Dark fix it.
- **Theme**: eight themes, each with a light and a dark side. See
  [themes](/GoMessages/themes/).
- **Background**: Gradient shows the theme's art; Solid is plain color.
- **Transparency**: how much of what's behind the window shows through.

## Notifications

- **Desktop notifications**: a banner when a message arrives.
- **Show message preview**: include the text in the banner. Turn it off if
  others can see your screen.
- **Notification sound**: play a sound for new messages. **Test** plays it.

To silence one conversation, open its **⋮** menu in the list and choose
**Mute**.

## Chats

- **Enter key sends**: when off, `Enter` adds a new line and `Cmd+Enter`
  sends.
- **Send typing indicators**: let people see when you're typing (RCS chats).

## Account

Shows which account you're paired with. **Unpair…** signs this computer
out.

GoMessages also remembers the window's size and place, and the text size
(`Cmd+=`, `Cmd+-`, `Cmd+0`).
