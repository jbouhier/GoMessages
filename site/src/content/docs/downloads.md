---
title: Download
description: Get GoMessages for your system.
---

Grab the build for your system from the
[latest release](https://github.com/jbouhier/GoMessages/releases/latest):

- **Mac** (Apple Silicon): `GoMessages-macos-aarch64.dmg`
- **Windows** (x64): `GoMessages-windows-x86_64.zip`
- **Linux** (x64): `GoMessages-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`

On Mac, copy `GoMessages.app` to `/Applications` and open it. Unsigned
builds need right-click, Open on first launch. Then continue with
[getting started](/GoMessages/getting-started/).
