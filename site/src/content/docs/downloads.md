---
title: Download
description: Get GoMessages for your computer.
---

Get the file for your computer from the
[latest release](https://github.com/jbouhier/GoMessages/releases/latest):

- **Mac** (Apple Silicon): `GoMessages-macos-aarch64.dmg`
- **Windows** (x64): `GoMessages-windows-x86_64.zip`
- **Linux** (x64): `GoMessages-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`

Then continue with [getting started](/GoMessages/getting-started/).
