Google Messages on your Mac, Linux, and Windows, as a real app. Your chats
in a native desktop window, paired with your Android phone, with persistent
login.

- **Keeps running** when you close the window, so messages keep arriving.
- **Unread count** on the Dock icon (Mac) and a dot on the menu bar / tray icon.
- **Menu bar or tray icon**, optional. On Mac you can hide the Dock icon.
- **Zoom** with `Cmd+=` / `Cmd+-` / `Cmd+0`, remembered across restarts.
- **Small and private**: native Rust, no Electron, no telemetry.

## Download

- **Mac** (Apple Silicon): `GoMessages-macos-aarch64.dmg` from the
  [latest release](https://github.com/jbouhier/GoMessages/releases/latest)
- **Windows** (x64): `GoMessages-windows-x86_64.zip` from the
  [latest release](https://github.com/jbouhier/GoMessages/releases/latest)
- **Linux** (x64): `GoMessages-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` from the
  [latest release](https://github.com/jbouhier/GoMessages/releases/latest)

## Install in a minute

1. Mac: copy `GoMessages.app` to `/Applications`. Windows: unzip and run the
   `.exe`. Linux: unpack and run the binary.
2. Sign in with Google, then tap the matching emoji on your phone.
3. Done, it stays signed in.
