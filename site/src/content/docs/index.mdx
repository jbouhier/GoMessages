Google Messages for Mac, Windows, and Linux. Your conversations in a fast,
keyboard-friendly desktop app, paired with your Android phone.

*Sample conversation from the app's demo mode.*

- **Everything you need**: photos and files, reactions, replies, typing
  indicators, archive, search, and new chats from your contacts.
- **Keyboard first**: `Cmd+K` jumps to anyone, and every action has a
  shortcut.
- **Themes**: eight of them, light and dark, with see-through windows if
  you like.
- **Notifications** with or without a preview, and mute per conversation.
- **Private**: no telemetry. Messages stay between your computer, Google,
  and your phone.

## Tour

Press `Cmd+K`, type a few letters, hit Enter.

<img src="/GoMessages/product/quick-switcher.jpg" alt="Jump to anyone with Cmd+K" width="2400" height="1500" />

Every action has a shortcut. `Cmd+/` lists them all.

<img src="/GoMessages/product/shortcuts.jpg" alt="Shortcuts for everything" width="2400" height="1500" />

Photos open full size. Zoom, copy, save, or flip through with the arrow keys.

<img src="/GoMessages/product/photos.jpg" alt="See it big" width="2400" height="1500" />

Pick a theme, light or dark, gradient or solid, see-through or not.

<img src="/GoMessages/product/settings.jpg" alt="Make it yours" width="2400" height="1500" />

No telemetry. Messages stay between your computer, Google, and your phone.

<img src="/GoMessages/product/privacy.jpg" alt="Your messages stay yours" width="2400" height="1500" />

## Get started

1. [Download](/GoMessages/downloads/) the app for your computer.
2. Choose **Sign in with Google**.
3. Tap the matching emoji on your phone. Done, it stays signed in.
