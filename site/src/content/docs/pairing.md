---
title: Pairing your phone
description: How GoMessages connects to your Android phone.
---

GoMessages shows what's on your phone's Google Messages app. Pairing links
the two over your Google account.

1. Open GoMessages on your Mac, Linux PC, or Windows PC.
2. Sign in with the same Google account used in Messages on your phone.
3. Your phone shows three emoji. Tap the one matching your computer screen.
4. Your phone vibrates when pairing completes.

Only one computer can be active at a time. If pairing asks about Bluetooth,
turn Bluetooth on, on both computer and phone, and keep them close. Pairing
verifies proximity.

If you don't use the app for a few weeks, Google unpairs it automatically.
Just pair again.
