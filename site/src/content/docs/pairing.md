---
title: Pairing your phone
description: How GoMessages connects to your Android phone.
---

GoMessages shows what's in Google Messages on your phone. Pairing links the
two through your Google account.

1. Open GoMessages and choose **Sign in with Google**.
2. A sign-in window opens. Sign in with the Google account you use in
   Messages on your phone.
3. The window closes and GoMessages shows an emoji.
4. Your phone shows a pairing prompt. Tap the same emoji.

No QR code or Bluetooth is needed. Your phone needs Google Messages and an
internet connection, and it needs to stay online: messages sync through it.

Google ends pairings it doesn't see used for a few weeks. If that happens,
pair again.

To unpair, for example on a shared computer, open **Settings → Account** and
choose **Unpair…**.
