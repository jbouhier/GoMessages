---
title: Themes
description: Change how GoMessages looks.
---

Open **Settings → Appearance**.

| Theme | Light side | Dark side |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default | Clean white, blue | Near black, soft blue |
| Graphite | Soft white, charcoal | Charcoal, silver |
| Sky | Morning sky with clouds | Deep blue |
| Mauve | Catppuccin Latte | Catppuccin Mocha |
| Aurora | Lilac dawn | Polar night |
| Peach | Warm blush | Embers |
| Amber | Sunrise | Sunset |
| Sage | Pale sage | Forest night |

**Mode** picks the side: System follows your computer, or fix it to Light or
Dark.

**Background** is Gradient, the theme's art behind the panels, or Solid,
plain color.

**Transparency** lets what's behind the window show through. On Mac and
Windows 11 it's blurred; on Linux the art fades to the theme's plain color.
Messages always stay solid so they're easy to read. With a busy wallpaper,
keep transparency low.

Every theme is checked so text stays readable on both sides. Want to make
your own? See the
[theming guide](https://github.com/jbouhier/GoMessages/blob/main/docs/THEMING.md).
